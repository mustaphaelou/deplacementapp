# Learning Record 0004: The Skills Landscape

**Date:** 2026-07-25

## What I learned

The Matt Pocock engineering skills system contains 35+ skills organized into categories: Foundation, Discovery & Research, Design & Planning, Critique, Building, Quality, Handoff, Specialized, Writing, and Meta. Every skill follows a predictable pattern (Trigger → Context Load → Procedure → Completion Criteria), and most are user-invoked — I decide when to use them.

## Key insight

Skills are designed to **chain** naturally. A typical workflow: `research` → `domain-modeling` → `grill-me` → `to-spec` → `to-tickets` → `implement` → `code-review` → `qa`. Understanding the categories helps me pick the right skill for each phase of work.
